Policy Overview

Our healthcare insurance policy is designed to provide financial protection and access to quality medical services. We aim to ensure that every individual receives timely and affordable healthcare without worrying about expenses. This policy covers a wide range of medical services, including consultations, diagnostics, hospitalization, and emergency care.

Coverage Benefits

  • ✔ Doctor consultations and specialist visits
  • ✔ Hospitalization and surgical procedures
  • ✔ Emergency and ambulance services
  • ✔ Diagnostic tests and lab services
  • ✔ Maternity and newborn care
  • ✔ Chronic disease management

Eligibility & Enrollment

Individuals aged 18 to 65 are eligible to enroll in this policy. Family plans are also available, allowing coverage for spouses and children under a single plan. Enrollment can be completed online through our platform with minimal documentation.

Claim Process

Our claim process is simple and transparent. Patients can avail cashless treatment at our network hospitals or apply for reimbursement by submitting medical bills and reports. Claims are processed quickly to ensure minimal delay in financial support.

Exclusions

  • ❌ Cosmetic or non-medical procedures
  • ❌ Self-inflicted injuries
  • ❌ Treatments not prescribed by certified doctors
  • ❌ Pre-existing conditions (within waiting period)
